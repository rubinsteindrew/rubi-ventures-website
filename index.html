Facet 02  ·  Also building

The Cube

Want a fun game to play with your friends? Give this one a roll.

The dead-simple drinking game my friends actually play. No accounts, no internet, no nonsense — just you, your table, and the roll of the cube.

  • Nine piles, three by three. Call higher or lower — three in a row and you pass it on.
  • Miss, and you drink for as many seconds as there are cards in that pile.
  • Add your players and start in seconds.
  • Works entirely offline — nothing ever leaves your phone.
  • No sign-up, no tracking, no ads.
  • Built for adults of legal drinking age. Please drink responsibly.
